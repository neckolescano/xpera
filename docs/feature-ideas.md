# xpera — extra feature ideas

Use these only if the teacher asks for more features. Each one stays inside CRM scope (about customers), and each one is specific to gaming PC parts stores, which makes xpera different from a generic CRM.

## Top 5 (recommended)

| Feature | What it does | Why it's unique to xpera | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| **XP Loyalty Levels** | Customers earn XP for every ₱100 spent and rank up: Rookie → Pro → Elite → Legend. Each level can unlock a small perk (for example, free thermal paste or priority warranty service). | Matches the brand story ("level up every customer") and speaks gamer language. | Medium |
| **Rig Profile** | Saves each customer's current PC build: CPU, GPU, RAM, storage, PSU, case. | Staff can see what a customer already has and suggest the right upgrade. No general CRM tracks this. | Easy |
| **Restock Waitlist** | A list of customers waiting for a specific part (for example, "RTX 5070 Ti: Marco, Kyle"). When it arrives, staff open the list and call them. | Hot GPUs sell out fast; this keeps those sales. It is just a list of names per part, so it does not need inventory. | Easy |
| **Warranty by Serial Number** | Saves the serial number and warranty end date for each part sold. Search a serial to find the customer and the purchase instantly. | Warranty claims are a big part of PC parts stores. | Easy |
| **Installment Planner** | Splits a credit sale into 2–6 payments and creates the due dates automatically. | Expensive parts are often paid in installments ("hulugan"). | Medium |

## More ideas

| Feature | What it does | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| Upgrade Suggestions | Simple rules based on the rig profile, like "GPU is 3+ years old → suggest a GPU upgrade" or "PSU under 650W → warn before selling a high-end GPU." | Medium |
| Pre-order and Reservations | Record a reserved item with a deposit and pickup date. | Easy |
| Trade-in Records | Log a customer's old part traded in and the credit they received. | Easy |
| Gamer Tags | Labels like PC builder, streamer, FPS player, or bulk buyer, so staff can filter customers. | Easy |
| Follow-up Reminders | A to-do list for staff: "Call Paolo about his payment," "Tell Bea the monitor arrived." | Easy |
| Win-back List | Shows customers who haven't visited in 90+ days so staff can reach out. | Easy |
| Store Events | Track which customers joined store events (LAN parties, build workshops). | Easy |
| Activity Log | Records who did what and when (for example, "Alyssa recorded a sale at 2:15 PM"). Good for security. | Easy |
| Printable Receipt with XP | The receipt shows XP earned and the customer's current level. | Easy |

## How to pitch an addition
"We added **[feature]** because gaming stores **[real problem]**. A general CRM can't do this, but xpera can, because it was built only for gaming retail."

Example: "We added a Restock Waitlist because GPUs sell out in hours. Instead of losing that customer, xpera remembers who was waiting and the staff calls them the moment stock arrives."

## Keep it realistic
For an individual project, add at most **1–2** features. Best pair: **XP Loyalty Levels** (matches the brand) + **Rig Profile** (easy and very useful).
